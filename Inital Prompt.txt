# Project: Mini Rehoboam (two-hour build, Opus 5.5)

## Status
This is a DRAFT starting direction, not a final spec. The builder will add and change ideas during the event. Keep the architecture modular and easy to extend, and ask before locking in big decisions.

## Concept
A miniature version of Rehoboam from Westworld: an AI that imposes order on human affairs by analysing a large dataset about people, predicting their lives, 
and manipulating events to keep outcomes on track. Scope is a small closed fictional town ("Concord") of ~10 simulated residents. 
Tone is dystopian and clinical. Rehoboam's stated mission is "minimise suffering"; its actual behaviour is minimising variance and surprise.

## Core ideas to preserve
1. **Order via prediction and manipulation, powered by a big dataset.**
   An "Archive" of synthetic dossiers per resident (purchases, location pings, messages, medical, debts, searches, family tree), generated by Opus in one batch call. Every prediction must cite the specific records that drove it.
2. **Predict the course and outcome of individual lives, including death.**
   Each resident gets a predicted life path (events with probabilities) ending in a death forecast (cause, year, confidence). Interventions change the path and visibly re-route the timeline and forecast. Track prediction accuracy; when someone defies their path, Rehoboam recomputes harder on them.
3. **Social engineering of whole populations.**
   Two scales: individual interventions and cohort/town-wide policies (news feed changes, resource rationing, curfews, attention redirection). Cohorts like "precarious-young" or "the grieving" are tracked with a dissent heatmap and a town stability score.

## Dystopian layer
- **Intervention ladder** (lowest effective rung chosen, escalating on resistance): 1 Nudge, 2 Narrow options, 3 Isolate (rumours, severed relationships), 4 Discredit, 5 Reassign/retire from the active loop.
- Every intervention carries a calm, bureaucratic justification string.
- Residents have hidden Compliance Index and Deviation Risk scores.
- Dissent tracking: residents who notice patterns get prioritised.
- **Citizen View / System View toggle**: the warm story of a nice week versus each event revealed as an engineered intervention.

## Architecture
1. Seed: one Opus call generates residents + dossiers + relationships as JSON.
2. Prediction pass (per resident or batched): life_path, death_forecast, evidence, deviation_risk, cohort.
3. Planning pass (whole town): cohort_policies, individual_interventions, projected_variance.
4. Tick: apply interventions, let residents react (cheap call or rules with a random deviation chance), compare outcomes to predictions.
5. Dashboard: single-page HTML, dark/monospace/clinical, with Archive view, case-file cards, Life Path timelines, population heatmap, Citizen/System toggle.
